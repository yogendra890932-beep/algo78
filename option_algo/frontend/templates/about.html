About Optiscalper

Rule-based options trading automation for the Indian market

In one line: Optiscalper is an automation platform that trades NSE/BSE index options in your own broker account using clearly defined, risk-first rules — and gives you a live terminal to watch, trail, and override every decision.

01What We Do

Discretionary options scalping is fast, emotional and hard to repeat. Optiscalper turns it into a process: market structure is read from the underlying and the option premium, a trade is only taken when several independent conditions agree, and every position is born with a stop-loss, a target and a defined risk budget.

The platform connects to your Upstox account. Orders are placed on your account, in your name, with your capital — we never hold funds and never take custody of your securities. You can stop the bot, pause new entries, trail a stop by hand, square off a position, or reduce a partial lot at any moment.

Automation

Strategies that stay disciplined

Trend-following, pullback continuation, breakout, EMA cross, VWAP bounce and range-fade setups, each evaluated against structure, volume and momentum.

Control

Your account, your call

Semi-auto approvals, manual entries from the terminal, live SL/target edits and instant square-off. The bot proposes; you always keep the override.

Visibility

A terminal, not a black box

Underlying and premium charts with independent timeframes, live P&L, position overlays, trade reasons and a complete audit of every fill.

Risk

Risk decided before entry

Per-trade stop-loss and R:R, daily loss limits, one pending trade at a time, no direction flips while a position is open, and a hard session end at 15:45 IST.

02How It Works

  1. Connect your broker. Link your Upstox app credentials once; the daily access token is refreshed automatically, without stopping your session.
  2. Choose a strategy and your risk. Pick the setups you want, your lot size, stop-loss percentage and reward-to-risk ratio.
  3. The engine reads the market. Live ticks build 1-minute candles for the index and the option premium; 5-minute structure sets the trend context.
  4. Signals are gated. A trade is taken only when the phase, direction and setup agree — and never late, never against an open position.
  5. You supervise. Watch it on the terminal, get push/Telegram/voice alerts, trail the stop or close it yourself whenever you want.

03Start in Paper Mode

Every new account gets a paper-trading trial. Paper mode runs the exact same engine, signals and exit logic as live trading, but records fills in a simulated book instead of sending them to your broker. It is the honest way to judge whether a setup — and its drawdown — suits you before any real money is involved.
