01Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how billing, refunds, and cancellations work for paid Optiscalper subscriptions ("the Service"). It forms part of, and should be read together with, our Terms of Service. By purchasing a subscription, you agree to this policy.

02Free Trial

New accounts may receive a free trial period that lets you evaluate the Service — including paper-trading mode — before paying. We strongly encourage you to use the trial and paper-trading mode to confirm the Service meets your needs before purchasing a paid plan. No payment is required to start a trial, and no refund is due for a trial because it is free.

03Subscription Plans & Billing

  • Paid plans are billed in advance for the duration you select (for example, monthly or quarterly).
  • Prices, features, and per-plan limits are shown at checkout and on the Billing page before you pay.
  • Unless you cancel, subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period using the payment method on file.
  • By subscribing, you authorise us and our payment processor to charge your selected payment method for the plan amount, on a recurring basis where applicable.

04When a Refund Is Available

We will issue a full refund in the following cases:

  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same subscription period.
  • Erroneous charge — an incorrect amount was charged due to a billing error on our side.
  • Failed service delivery — you were charged but the paid features were not activated or were materially unavailable for the entire paid period due to a fault on our side (and we were unable to restore them).
  • Unauthorised transaction — a charge was made without your authorisation, verified by us and/or your bank.
  • Statutory rights — where a refund is required by applicable consumer protection law.

At our sole discretion, we may also offer a pro-rata or goodwill refund in other limited circumstances. Any such offer does not create an obligation to do so in future cases.

05When a Refund Is Not Available

Please read carefully.

  • Trading losses — refunds do not cover, and are not related to, any profit or loss from trades placed through the Service. Trading outcomes are your responsibility (see the Risk Disclosure in our Terms of Service).
  • Change of mind — you no longer wish to use the Service after a paid period has started.
  • Partial use — you did not use the Service, or used it for only part of a billing period.
  • Auto-renewal — a renewal charge where you did not cancel before the renewal date.
  • Broker/third-party issues — losses or downtime caused by Upstox, your internet provider, exchange outages, or other third parties outside our control.
  • Account termination for breach — accounts suspended or terminated for violating our Terms, abusive behaviour, or security risk.
  • Completed billing periods — periods that have already elapsed are not refunded on a pro-rata basis on cancellation.

06How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email support@optiscalper.com from your registered email address and include:

  1. Your account email and, if available, your subscription/invoice reference,
  2. The date and amount of the charge,
  3. The reason for the request, and
  4. Any supporting details (for example, a bank statement line or transaction ID).

Please submit refund requests within 7 days of the charge. Requests made after this window may still be reviewed but are less likely to be approved, except where required by law.

07Processing Time & Method

  • We aim to review refund requests within 3–5 business days of receiving them.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; we cannot redirect a refund to a different account or method.
  • Once initiated by us, banks and payment processors typically take 5–10 business days to credit the amount. This timing is outside our control.
  • Refunds are processed in the same currency as the original charge. Any currency-conversion differences or bank fees are not reimbursed.

08Failed & Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were charged twice, or a payment failed but your account was still debited, contact us first at support@optiscalper.com. In most cases the amount is either auto-reversed by the payment processor within a few business days, or we will arrange a refund once we confirm the duplicate/erroneous charge.

09Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the Billing page in your account, or by emailing us. When you cancel:

  • Your subscription will not renew at the end of the current billing period.
  • You retain access to paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • No further charges are made after cancellation takes effect.

Cancellation stops future billing; it does not trigger a refund for the current period except as described in Section 04.

10Chargebacks & Disputes

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. Most concerns can be resolved quickly by our support team. If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us first, we may suspend the account while the dispute is investigated, and we reserve the right to contest disputes we believe are unfounded. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in permanent account termination.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The policy in effect at the time of your purchase governs that purchase.
